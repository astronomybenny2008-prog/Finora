Insurance shouldn't feel like a dictionary.

Understand important insurance terms without complicated language.

💳

Premium

The amount a policyholder pays to maintain insurance coverage, according to the policy terms (e.g. ₹8,500/year or ₹850/month).

🛡️

Coverage

The specific risks, events, treatments, or situations protected by the insurance policy against financial losses.

📄

Policy

The legal contract and formal agreement between the policyholder and the authorised insurance company.

📝

Claim

A formal request submitted to the insurer asking for reimbursement or payout when a covered event occurs.

💰

Deductible

The pre-agreed amount you pay out-of-pocket before insurance coverage covers the remaining cost (e.g. ₹5,000).

🚫

Exclusion

Specific events, conditions, treatments, or hazards that are explicitly NOT covered by the insurance policy.

⏳

Waiting Period

The time duration after purchasing a policy before certain benefits or pre-existing condition cover become active.

🎯

Sum Insured

The maximum guaranteed financial protection limit the insurer agrees to pay during the policy year (e.g. ₹10 Lakhs).

📅

Policy Term

The designated duration of time for which the insurance policy provides active coverage (e.g. 1 Year, 10 Years, 30 Years).

🔄

Renewal

Extending or continuing the insurance policy coverage for another cycle by paying the renewal premium before due date.

Complex Policy Language Clause 14.B (Hospitalization)

“The Underwriter shall indemnify the Insured for Reasonable and Customary Charges incurred during Inpatient Hospitalization exceeding twenty-four (24) continuous hours, subject to the Specified Deductible of ₹10,000 and the Initial 30-Day Moratorium Period, excluding Pre-Existing Ailments until the expiry of twenty-four (24) consecutive months of continuous coverage without interruption.”

⚠️ High cognitive friction · Jargon heavy
✨ AI
Finora AI Plain Explanation Easy to Understand
  • Hospital Stay: You must be admitted in hospital for at least 24 hours to claim medical costs.
  • Your Share: You pay the first ₹10,000 of the total bill out of pocket (deductible). The insurer covers the rest up to your sum insured.
  • New Illnesses: Regular sickness claims start after the first 30 days of policy start date (accidents covered from Day 1).
  • Pre-existing Conditions: Existing health conditions will only be covered after you hold this policy continuously for 2 years.

02
📤

03
🔍
