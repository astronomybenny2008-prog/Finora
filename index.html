What documents do I need for a home loan, and how is eligibility evaluated?

Here is a simplified breakdown of the Home Loan journey:

Eligibility is typically assessed using your stable monthly income, credit profile (CIBIL/Equifax), and existing debt obligations (FOIR). Essential documents usually include: 1) Proof of Identity & Residence, 2) Last 3-6 months' salary slips or ITR for self-employed, 3) 6 months' bank statements, and 4) Property chain documents.

AI guidance is informational and does not replace advice or decisions from regulated financial institutions.
