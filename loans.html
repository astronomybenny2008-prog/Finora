      Key Assessment Factors

        📋

        Comprehensive Document Checklist

        Standard Indian lending paperwork guidelines

        1. Identity & KYC Proof

        ✓ PAN Card (Mandatory) / Aadhaar Card / Passport / Voter ID

        2. Address & Residence Proof

        ✓ Aadhaar Card / Electricity Bill (within 3 months) / Registered Rent Agreement

        3. Income & Financial Records

        ✓ Latest 3–6 months salary slips / Last 6 months bank account statements / Form 16 or 2–3 years ITR

        4. Asset / Collateral Specifics

        ✓ Home Loan: Sale deed, approved building plan, NOC, Encumbrance Certificate (EC)
        ✓ Vehicle Loan: Proforma dealer quotation / invoice
        ✓ Education Loan: College admission letter and detailed fee schedule
