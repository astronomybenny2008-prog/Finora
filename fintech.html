Digital Safety & Fraud Prevention Rules

Essential practices for navigating digital financial apps.

Never Share UPI PIN

UPI PIN is only required to SEND money, never to RECEIVE funds or claim rewards.

Verify QR Codes

Always confirm merchant name before completing any scanned QR payment.

Official App Stores

Install banking and fintech apps exclusively from Google Play or Apple App Store.

Report Instant Block

Use national helpline 1930 immediately if you spot any unauthorized transaction.
